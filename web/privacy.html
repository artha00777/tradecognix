Privacy policy

TradeCognix is run by DevCognix. This page explains what we collect when you use the site and the Studio, why, who else touches it, and how to have it corrected or deleted. We don't sell your data and we don't run advertising trackers.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password, which we store only as a salted hash.
  • Broker connection details: the API key, secret, client ID or access token you enter for a broker. These are encrypted before they are saved and are used only to talk to that broker on your instruction.
  • What you build: strategy text, parsed rules, backtest settings and results, paper-trading sessions and their orders.
  • Waitlist: the email you give us when you join the Pro or Elite waitlist.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and request times, kept in server logs for security, rate limiting and fixing faults.

We don't ask for your PAN, Aadhaar, bank details or card numbers. Payments are switched off during the beta.

Why we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To turn your strategy text into rules, run backtests and paper trades, and show you the results.
  • To connect to your broker when you ask us to, for prices, account data and, once enabled, orders.
  • To tell you when paid plans open, if you joined the waitlist.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We process this data on the basis of the consent you give when you sign up or submit a form, under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You can withdraw consent at any time by asking us to delete your account.

Who else handles it

We use a small number of service providers to run TradeCognix. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Render hosts the application server.
  • Supabase hosts the database.
  • GitHub Pages serves this website.
  • An AI language-model provider may receive the text of a strategy you type, without your email or broker details, to help turn it into rules. If it is unavailable we use our own rule-based parser.
  • Your broker receives the requests you authorise when you connect an account.

Some of these providers store data on servers outside India. We don't sell or rent personal data, and we share it with authorities only when the law requires it.

Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps your sign-in token and your light or dark theme choice. Clearing site data in your browser removes them.

How long we keep it

We keep account data, strategies and results while your account is open. When you ask us to delete your account we erase it, except where the law requires us to keep something for longer. Security logs are kept for at least one year, as the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 require.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or name someone to exercise these rights for you if you can't. You can also raise a grievance with us.

Email contact@devcognix.com or message us on WhatsApp at +91 90190 88160.

We aim to reply within 7 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, broker credentials are encrypted at rest, and access to production systems is limited. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Age

TradeCognix is for people aged 18 and over. Trading accounts with Indian brokers need you to be an adult.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell signed-in users. The operator is DevCognix.
