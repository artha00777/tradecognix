During the beta

  • All current features are free.
  • Pro and Elite are free during the beta for people on the waitlist. Joining the waitlist costs nothing and you can leave it at any time by emailing us.
  • We never ask for card or bank details in the beta. If anyone asks you to pay for TradeCognix, please tell us.

When paid plans start

Before we charge for any plan, we will publish its price, billing period, cancellation and refund terms on this page and ask you to accept them. Nobody on the free plan or the waitlist will be moved to a paid plan automatically.

Broker and exchange charges

Brokerage, STT, exchange fees, GST, stamp duty and any broker API subscriptions are charged by your broker, not by TradeCognix, and are covered by your broker's own refund rules.
