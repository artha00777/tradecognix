What TradeCognix is

TradeCognix is a software tool. It turns trading ideas you describe into rules, backtests them, paper-trades them and, where enabled, sends orders through a broker account you connect. We are not a stockbroker, investment adviser or research analyst registered with SEBI, and nothing in the service is investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Every strategy is yours: you decide what to run and you are responsible for the result.

Beta service

The service is in beta. Features may change, break or be withdrawn, and results may contain errors. Backtests may use generated practice prices and estimated option premiums; the Studio labels these results. Live trading is switched off for public users during the beta.

Broker connections

When you connect a broker you authorise us to use the credentials you provide to fetch data and, where enabled, place and cancel orders for that account. Your broker's own terms, charges, limits and exchange rules also apply. Brokers and exchanges can reject, delay or change orders, and may need a registered static IP or a daily login; we can't control that. Remove a connection at any time from the Brokers screen or by revoking the API key at your broker.
