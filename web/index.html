How it works

From a sentence to a live strategy, in four deliberate steps.

01

Connect your broker

Link your account with a secure API key and TOTP. Keys are encrypted at rest and never shared.

02

Describe it in plain English

Write the rules the way you'd explain them to a friend. We turn them into precise, editable conditions.

03

Backtest with real costs

Every trade pays slippage, brokerage, STT, exchange charges, GST and stamp duty. See gross against net P&L, win rate, profit factor, expectancy and max drawdown, with whipsaw detection.

04

Paper trade, then go live

FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need to know coding?

No. You describe the strategy in plain English and edit the rules we extract. Zero lines of code.

Which brokers are supported?

Angel One (SmartAPI) is live. Zerodha, Upstox, Groww, Dhan, Fyers, ICICI Direct, Kotak Neo, 5paisa, Alice Blue, Shoonya (Finvasia) and Motilal Oswal are rolling out.

Is my money safe?

Your funds stay in your broker account. We never hold money; we only place orders through your API.

Is live trading automatic?

Only after you explicitly enable it with a two-step switch. You can hit the kill switch at any time.

What about SEBI's algo trading rules?

TradeCognix is built to align with SEBI's retail algo framework: broker-approved strategies, static IP whitelisting and order tagging.
