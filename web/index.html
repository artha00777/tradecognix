FAQ

Straight answers.

Something else? contact@devcognix.com

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe the strategy in plain English and review the rules we extract. Every rule is editable before you backtest.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. Describe a strategy and run a backtest straight away. You only create a free account when you want to paper trade, so your sessions and journal are saved.

Which brokers are supported?

Angel One (SmartAPI) is live. Zerodha, Upstox, Dhan, Fyers, Groww, ICICI Direct, Kotak Neo, 5paisa, Alice Blue, Shoonya (Finvasia) and Motilal Oswal are rolling out.

Is my money safe?

Your funds stay in your broker account. TradeCognix never holds money; it only places orders through your broker's API. API credentials are encrypted at rest.

How realistic are the backtest costs?

Each simulated trade pays brokerage, STT, exchange transaction charges, SEBI fees, stamp duty, GST and slippage. Results show gross and net separately, and flag when data is synthetic or option premiums are estimated.

Is live trading automatic?

Only after you explicitly switch it on with a two-step confirmation. The kill switch stops every strategy and squares off open positions at once.

What about SEBI's algo trading rules?

TradeCognix is built to align with SEBI's retail algo framework: orders go through your own broker, every order is tagged to its strategy, and kill switches and loss caps are always on. Under NSE's 20 Nov 2026 circular, a personal static IP is only for traders who code their own algos, so live trading for everyone else is enabled through partner brokers' servers as they come on board.
