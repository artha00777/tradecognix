What people ask before connecting a broker.

Didn’t see yours? Write to contact@devcognix.com and a person will reply.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You write the strategy in plain English and check the rules we pull out of it. Anything we got wrong, you edit before the backtest runs.

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. Write a strategy and backtest it straight away. You only need a free account once you start paper trading, because that’s when there are sessions and a journal worth saving.

Which brokers are supported?

Angel One (SmartAPI) works today. Zerodha, Upstox, Dhan, Fyers, Groww, ICICI Direct, Kotak Neo, 5paisa, Alice Blue, Shoonya (Finvasia) and Motilal Oswal are being added one at a time.

Is my money safe?

Your funds stay in your broker account. TradeCognix never holds money. It only places orders through your broker’s API, and your API credentials are encrypted at rest.

How realistic are the backtest costs?

Each simulated trade pays brokerage, STT, exchange transaction charges, SEBI fees, stamp duty, GST and slippage. Gross and net are shown separately, and the results tell you when data is synthetic or option premiums are estimated rather than real.

Is live trading automatic?

Only after you switch it on yourself with a two-step confirmation. The kill switch stops every strategy and squares off open positions in one go.

What about SEBI's algo trading rules?

TradeCognix is designed around SEBI’s retail algo framework. Orders go through your own broker, every order is tagged to its strategy, and kill switches and loss caps are always on. Under NSE’s 20 Nov 2026 circular, a personal static IP is only for traders who code their own algos, so live trading for everyone else is enabled through partner brokers’ servers as they come on board.
